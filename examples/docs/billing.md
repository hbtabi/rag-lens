# Billing and Plans

Lumen Cloud bills every workspace monthly in arrears. Usage is metered per second for compute and per
gigabyte-month for storage, and invoices are issued on the first working day of each month.

## Plans

The Starter plan is free and includes 2 vCPU-hours per day, 5 GB of object storage and community support.
The Team plan costs £24 per seat per month and adds SSO, audit logs and 99.9% uptime commitments.
Enterprise pricing is negotiated annually and includes a named technical account manager.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards, as well as SEPA and BACS direct debit for annual
contracts. Invoices can be paid by bank transfer within 30 days on the Enterprise plan.

## Refunds

Refunds are available within 14 days of an annual upgrade if fewer than 10% of included credits have
been used. Monthly plans are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end
of the billing period.

## Spending limits

Workspace owners can set a hard spending cap under Settings → Billing. When 80% of the cap is reached an
email alert is sent; at 100% new deployments are paused while running services keep serving traffic.
