# Incident Response

We publish real-time platform health at status.lumen.example and post updates at least every 30 minutes
during an active incident.

## Severity levels

SEV1 means a full outage or data loss affecting many customers and pages the on-call engineer
immediately. SEV2 is a major degradation with a workaround. SEV3 covers minor bugs with limited impact.

## Uptime commitments

The Team plan commits to 99.9% monthly uptime and Enterprise to 99.95%. If we miss the commitment you
receive service credits: 10% of the monthly fee below the target and 25% below 99.0%.

## Post-incident reviews

Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident gets a blameless post-incident review published within five working days,
including a timeline, root cause and follow-up actions.
